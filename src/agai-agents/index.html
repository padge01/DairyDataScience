agAI · farm agents

Agents that own a result on your dairy

Each agAI agent is given one part of the dairy to look after. It reads every load of your records, judges the newest 30 days against your own farm's year, and brings a finding only when something has moved: what it is, the cows or pens behind it, the likely cause, and what to check first.

An owner for each result

Fresh cows, deaths and culls, breeding, milk quality, feeding, calves, money and daily work each have an agent that answers for them.

Watches every load

Each new day of DairyComp, milk and feed records is read as it arrives. Nothing waits for a monthly report.

Finding, cause and cows

A finding names the cows, pens or groups behind it, how sure it is, and the cause that stands out. It stays open until the number comes back.

Quiet when the farm is fine

No finding is made up to fill the page. Where the records don't hold what an agent needs, it says so plainly.

Cards marked Measured are real readings on agAI's two demo dairies, Larkspur Flats and Mesa Palomar, on 4 November 2026. Cards marked Example show what a finding looks like; their figures are illustrations, not measurements.

agAI reads only what the farm records. Lameness, days open, labour and drug costs are not priced where no costs are recorded. An agent that cannot read a farm says why on its own line.
